Our Story

The story
behind every
piece

What began as a quiet love for tiny, intricate things has grown into Svarci Handicrafts — a small studio where every miniature scene, every paper curl and every stitched figure carries a piece of me. I was drawn to handwork long before I called it a passion: rolling my first quilling strip, shaping my first clay bead, painting my first kolam manai. There is something honest about making by hand. The slowness teaches patience, the repetition teaches reverence, and the finished piece always carries the warmth of the hours spent on it.

So much of what I make comes from the things I grew up around — pongal panai on auspicious mornings, kolam at the doorstep, the warm clutter of a petti kadai, the bright joy of return-gift hampers at weddings and house-warmings. I try to bottle those memories at miniature scale, or fold them into a personalised gift made for someone you love. Every order is treated as one — never mass produced, always given the time it deserves. If you ever wonder whether real care goes into these pieces, the answer is yes — every minute of it, with love.

How it works

My Creative Process

01
Ideate
Every piece begins with an idea — a sketch, a mood, an occasion. I spend time planning proportions, colours and materials before picking up a single tool.
02
Design
From sketch to structure — I map out the design carefully, selecting the right papers, clays, threads or materials to bring the vision to life.
03
Craft
This is where the magic happens. Hours of patient, meticulous handwork — rolling quills, shaping miniatures, beading jewellery, painting kolam manai — until every detail feels just right.
04
Deliver
Each piece is carefully packaged and sent with love. For custom orders, I share progress photos so you're part of the journey from start to finish.
